Jistra Adventures

For Jistra Adventures, an outdoor adventure company based in Premantura, we created a dynamic film showcasing the natural beauty and adventurous spirit of southern Istria. Through coastal landscapes, outdoor activities and experiences around Cape Kamenjak, our goal was to capture the freedom, excitement and authenticity of exploring the region.
